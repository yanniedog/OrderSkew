Quick Setup Sequence

  1. 1

    Choose a trading mode: buy-only, sell-only, or buy + sell.

  2. 2

    Set your range using percentage width or fixed floor/ceiling targets.

  3. 3

    Select order count and spacing type: absolute for equal gaps or relative for geometric gaps.

    Adjust capital skew to control how aggressively capital is weighted across rungs.

  5. 5

    Check projected outcomes in the chart and then validate each rung in the table.

Trading Modes Cheat Sheet
Buy Only

Accumulate below market with pre-defined staggered bids.

Sell Only

Scale out above market when you already hold inventory.

Buy + Sell

Bracket both sides to structure a full cycle around a mid price.

Risk Checklist Before Executing
  • Confirm total capital and fees before sizing up.
  • Validate floor/ceiling so no rung sits outside your thesis.
  • Check spacing mode because geometric spacing widens quickly.
  • Use smaller size while testing and refine after real fills.
